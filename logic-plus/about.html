About Logic Plus

A technically focused electrical contracting partner built around experienced people, disciplined execution and dependable support.

Who We Are

Engineering capability with practical site experience

Logic Plus Electrical Contracting LLC is an Abu Dhabi-based electrical contracting company serving project, facility and infrastructure requirements.

Our team includes experienced managers, qualified engineers by ADDC & ADQCC, CAD design engineers and operators, professional staff, skilled supervisors and labourers. This combination allows us to coordinate engineering, site execution, testing, commissioning and ongoing electrical support.

We focus on understanding client requirements, planning the work carefully and delivering with attention to quality, safety, coordination and handover.
