HSE Policy

Responsible electrical work starts with protecting people, controlling risks and maintaining a strong safety culture.

Our Commitment

Safety is integral to professional electrical delivery.

Logic Plus recognizes the importance of protecting employees, clients, visitors, project stakeholders and the environment throughout our activities.

Our HSE approach emphasizes hazard awareness, competent supervision, safe electrical work practices, appropriate training, emergency preparedness, responsible environmental practices and continual improvement.

  • Identify hazards and control workplace risks.
  • Promote safe electrical work and site practices.
  • Provide appropriate training and safety awareness.
  • Maintain competent supervision and responsible work planning.
  • Reduce waste and avoid unnecessary environmental impact.
  • Review performance and improve practices over time.
HSEPeople. Safety. Responsibility.

A disciplined approach to safer and more responsible project execution.

Core Principles

Four pillars of our HSE approach

01

Protect People

Prioritize the health and safety of everyone affected by our work.

02

Control Risk

Recognize hazards and apply appropriate controls before work proceeds.

03

Respect Environment

Use responsible practices to reduce waste, pollution and unnecessary impact.

04

Improve Continuously

Learn from experience and strengthen HSE performance over time.

Safe Work Culture

Good planning is part of good safety.

Clear scope, competent people, appropriate controls and effective communication help create safer working environments.
